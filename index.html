Beyond the Classroom

Organizations & Teams

SEDS — R&D Liquid Engine Rocket

Engine Systems Subteam

Member of the Engine Systems subteam on Torch Ignitor, SEDS's R&D team building a liquid-propellant rocket engine, working on the ignition and control hardware.

As a member of the Engine subteam, I developed robust data acquisition (DAQ) systems and assembled igniter control boxes for the testing sequence. By integrating a LabJack device and Synnax telemetry dashboards for real-time monitoring, I successfully increased sensor read rates from 100Hz, with plans to reach 10,000Hz through ongoing firmware optimization. Additionally, I assisted with critical piping and instrumentation diagram (P&ID) updates to ensure safe and reliable engine testing operations.

UNLV Formula Racing

Battery & BMS

Built a battery pack for the team's electric race vehicle, then tested and debugged its Battery Management System (BMS) over CAN bus.

Contributed to the physical assembly of the electrical accumulator battery pack while rigorously monitoring high-voltage wiring standards and competition regulations. Based on this safety review, I advocated for a critical design change to reduce cell sizes. After modeling the proposed layout in SolidWorks, I proved that all high-voltage cell spacing requirements could still be safely met, successfully reducing the total cell volume by 4 cubic inches and optimizing the footprint for future iterations.
